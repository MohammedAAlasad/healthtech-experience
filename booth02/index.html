Five tools

1. Connected Papers

Turns one paper into a visual map of related work.

Best for: finding the main papers around a topic fast.

connectedpapers.com

2. ResearchRabbit

Builds collections of papers and suggests similar ones and authors.

Best for: keeping a reading list that grows with you.

researchrabbit.ai

3. Litmaps

Maps how papers cite each other over time and tracks new ones.

Best for: following a topic as new papers appear.

litmaps.com

4. Citation Gecko

Starts from a few seed papers and finds the ones they cite and are cited by.

Best for: snowballing from papers you already trust.

citationgecko.com

5. Inciteful

Analyzes a citation network to surface key and recent papers.

Best for: spotting the most influential papers in a field.

inciteful.xyz

Free-tier limits change. Check each site for current terms.

Try it in 4 steps

  1. Pick one paper from your course.
  2. Ask Claude to summarize it and list its key references.
  3. Paste the paper title into Connected Papers or ResearchRabbit.
  4. Compare: does the map match what Claude told you?

Always verify

AI can invent references. Look up each DOI and open the paper to confirm it says what Claude says. Install skills and connectors only from sources you trust.
